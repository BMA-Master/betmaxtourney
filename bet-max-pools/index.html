String wins together to tier up. Each consecutive win advances you Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum. Higher tiers multiply every payout (1×, 1.5×, 2×, 3×). Lose and you reset.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Free pools are free forever. Bet Max Commish tools unlock bigger lobbies, longer pools, and advanced game modes.

Do my friends need to sign up?

Yes, but signup is free and takes about 30 seconds. You will send them a unique invite link to join your pool.

What sports can I include in a pool?

NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, NCAA Football and Basketball, EPL and other top soccer leagues, MMA, boxing, golf, cricket, and more. Free pools support up to 2 sports; Bet Max Commish unlocks all available sports.

Can I edit a pool after it's started?

Limited edits are allowed once players have joined. You can update the description and invite more players, but core settings like sports, dates, and game mode lock in to keep things fair.

What is a Bet Max Commish?

A Bet Max Commish is the pool host. Free gives every host 1 active pool, up to 10 players, 2 sports, and 7-day max length on the default game mode. Bet Max Commish tools remove those limits, add advanced game modes like Bracket, Survivor, and Squads, and let you run multiple pools simultaneously.

How do I cancel Bet Max Commish?

Anytime. Manage Bet Max Commish access from your account settings with no long-term commitment.

Can I use Bet Max Pools for paid competitions?

No. Bet Max Tourney is 100% free-to-play. Pools award trophies and bragging rights only.
