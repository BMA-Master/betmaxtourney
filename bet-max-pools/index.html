Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Pools are free forever. No entry fees, no rake, no catch.

Do my friends need to sign up?

Yes, but signup is free and takes about 30 seconds. You'll send them a unique invite link to join your pool.

What sports can I include in a pool?

NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, NCAA Football and Basketball, EPL and other top soccer leagues, MMA, boxing, golf, cricket, and more.

Can I edit a pool after it's started?

Limited edits are allowed once players have joined. You can update the description and invite more players, but core settings like sports, dates, and game mode lock in to keep things fair.

Can I use Bet Max Pools for paid competitions?

No. Bet Max Tourney is 100% free-to-play. Pools award trophies and bragging rights only.
