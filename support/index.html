Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Is Bet Max Tourney really free?

Yes. Join any tournament without entry fees, deposits, or payment setup. Bet Max Tourney is 100% free to play.

What are TD$ (Tournament Dollars)?

TD$ is the in-game scoring currency. Every player starts each tournament with the same balance, places bets to grow it, and the highest total at the end wins. TD$ are not real money.

How do I create a private pool for my friends?

Head to Bet Max Pools, pick a format and sports, and share the invite link. Free forever.

I can't log in / something looks broken.

Check the status page first — if everything is green, email support@betmaxtourney.com with a screenshot and the device you're using.
