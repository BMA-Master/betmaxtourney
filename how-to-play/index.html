Currency

What are Tournament Dollars (TD$)?

TD$ is in-game currency used exclusively within tournaments. It is not real money. It is how we track your performance and ranking.

  • Starting Balance: Varies by tournament.
  • Purpose: Wager on picks to grow your stack and reinvest winnings throughout the tournament.
  • Goal: Have the highest TD$ balance when the tournament ends.
  • Tournament Rule: Wager your entire TD$ balance over the course of the tournament to qualify for final rankings.
  • Important: TD$ cannot be withdrawn or converted to real money.

Bet Max Tourney: In Three Steps.

This is the main Bet Max Tourney flow: join a free tournament, put TD$ behind your reads, and climb as the games settle.

01

Join a free tournament.

Browse live and upcoming tournaments across supported sports. Everyone enters with the same starting TD$ balance, so the leaderboard starts clean.

Mode Guides

Learn each mode when you need it.

Set It and Forget It Build the card once. No live adjustments.
  • Choose the slate. Enter the tournament and review every game before anything locks.
  • Lock your card. Submit all bets before the first game. Once the slate starts, your card is set.
  • Track the finish. Watch results settle and see who built the strongest pre-game position.
Longshot Mayhem Underdog reads carry more leaderboard weight.
  • Find the mispriced side. Scan the board for underdogs and spots where the field may be too safe.
  • Take a bigger swing. Risk TD$ where the reward can actually change your rank.
  • Jump if it hits. Longshot hits can move you past safer players who only followed the favorite.
Bet Max Tiers Win streak unlocks higher payout multipliers.
  • Start at Bronze. Every player begins at the base tier with a 1× payout multiplier on every winning bet.
  • String wins to tier up. Each consecutive win advances you Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum (1×, 1.5×, 2×, 3×).
  • One loss resets. A losing bet drops you back to Bronze. Higher tiers reward conviction and timing.
Bet Max Survivor Stay alive round after round.
  • Make the survival choice. Select the side required by the tournament rules before the round locks.
  • Get it right. A correct choice keeps you alive. A miss ends your run.
  • Outlast the field. Advance until only the strongest entry remains.
Bet Max Bracket Win the matchup, advance the bracket. Bet Max Commish
  • Enter the bracket. Join a head-to-head path where each round has a direct opponent.
  • Win the round. Use your TD$ better than the player across from you.
  • Advance or exit. Keep winning matchups until the final bracket spot is yours.
Bet Max Squads Compete solo, rank as a team. Bet Max Commish
  • Build the squad. Create or join a team before entering the tournament.
  • Each player competes. Every squad member manages their own TD$ balance.
  • The team total ranks. Squad position is determined by the combined performance of the group.
Reading the Board

Betting Odds Explained

Pick Outcomes

Win

Your pick was correct. Earn TD$ based on the odds.

Example: Bet 100 TD$ at +150 → Win 150 TD$ profit.

Lose

Your pick was wrong. Lose the TD$ you wagered.

Example: Bet 100 TD$ → Lose 100 TD$.

Push

The result lands exactly on the line. Get your TD$ back.

Example: Bet 100 TD$ on Over 45, final score 45 → Get 100 TD$ back.

Understanding Odds (Simplified)

Odds tell you how much TD$ you can win. We use American odds format.

+

Positive Odds

+150, +200, +300

These are underdogs. The number shows how much you win if you bet 100 TD$.

Example: Bet 100 TD$ at +150. If you win, get back 100 TD$ + 150 TD$ profit = 250 TD$ total.

−

Negative Odds

-110, -150, -200

These are favorites. The number shows how much you need to bet to win 100 TD$.

Example: Bet 110 TD$ at -110. If you win, get back 110 TD$ + 100 TD$ profit = 210 TD$ total.

Quick Rule: Higher positive numbers (+300) = bigger underdog, bigger payout. Higher negative numbers (-200) = bigger favorite, smaller payout.
Common Terms

Bet Max Glossary

Keep this as a quick reference. The full rules for any tournament live inside that tournament's details.

Core Terms
TD$
Tournament Dollars. The in-game scoring balance used to place bets and rank players.
Leaderboard
The live ranking table for a tournament.
Starting Balance
The amount of TD$ every player receives at entry.
Settled Bet
A bet with a final result that has been added to or removed from your balance.
Markets & Odds
Spread
A margin-based market where one side gives or receives points.
Total
An over/under market based on the combined final score or stat.
Moneyline
A market based on which side wins the game outright.
Prop
A market based on a specific player, team, or game event.
Favorite
The side expected by the odds to be more likely to win.
Underdog
The side expected by the odds to be less likely to win.
Push
A tied market result where the risked amount is returned.
Tournament Terms
Slate
The set of games included in a tournament or round.
Lock
The deadline when a bet, card, or mode decision can no longer be changed.
Round
A stage inside bracket, survivor, or multi-part tournament formats.
Pool
A private competition created for a specific group.
Bet Max Commish
The host who creates and manages a Bet Max Pool.
