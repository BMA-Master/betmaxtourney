Account & privacy

Delete your Bet Max Tourney account

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data. No app download or sign-in is needed to send a request.

Form not loading? Send your deletion request to info@bmadevs.com. Never include your password.

After you send a request

We’ll contact you at the email provided to verify account ownership and follow up.

Submitting this form does not automatically delete your account.

Prefer to delete it yourself?

You can also delete your account on the website or in the app.

  1. Sign in and open Profile & Learn → Account & privacy.
  2. Select Delete Account.
  3. Enter your current password and confirm.
Open account settings →

What happens to your information

Deletion is permanent. You will be signed out and any remaining Tournament Dollars (TD$) will be forfeited. TD$ have no cash value.

When deletion completes, your account name, email, username, credentials, chat message text, invitations, inbox, notification preferences, and registered notification devices are removed from the active game database. Your profile is replaced with Deleted Player.

De-identified tournament entries, picks, results, published contest rules, and the date, category, and outcome of audit events or abuse reports may remain for competition integrity, security, and dispute resolution. These records may retain an internal account reference and do not have a fixed automatic expiration. Personal account payloads are removed from associated application audit and account-email records.

Helpdesk correspondence is stored separately. Include a request to remove it if you also want your support inquiries deleted; we may retain information needed to resolve an open issue or meet a security or legal obligation.

See our Privacy Policy for details about data retention and privacy requests.

The request form is provided by Mach Five Magnet. Information you submit is used to process and verify your deletion request, not for marketing.

Delete Helpdesk data while keeping your account

To request deletion of your Helpdesk correspondence without deleting your Bet Max Tourney account, email info@bmadevs.com from the address used for your inquiry. State that you want to keep your game account. We may need to verify ownership and may retain information needed for an open issue or a security or legal obligation. Never send your password.
