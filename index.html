How to Compete

At the end of each tournament, standings are determined by highest TD$ (Tournament Dollars) balance:

1

Join a Free Tournament

Browse live tournaments across NFL, NBA, MLB and more. No entry fee, no credit card required. See who's competing and join instantly.

2

Make Your Picks & Compete

Everyone starts with equal TD$. Allocate your budget across picks—spreads, totals, moneylines, and player props. Assign confidence levels to your picks. Higher confidence = more TD$ at risk.

3

Climb the Leaderboard

Watch your rank update live. Track TD$ balances in real-time. How you allocate your budget is your strategy—compete to finish with the highest balance.
