Is Bet Max Tourney really free?

Yes. Join any tournament without entry fees, deposits, or payment setup. Bet Max Tourney is 100% free to play.

What are TD$ (Tournament Dollars)?

TD$ is the in-game scoring currency. Every player starts each tournament with the same TD$ balance, places bets to grow it, and the highest balance at the end wins. TD$ are not real money.

How do live leaderboards work?

Leaderboards update in real time as your TD$ bets settle and game results come in. Watch your rank shift live as games play out.

Can I play with just my friends?

Yes. Bet Max Pools lets you create a free private tournament and invite your crew into the same scoreboard. Just you and your friends competing for bragging rights.

What kind of game modes are available?

Six formats: Bet Max Tourney (classic free-form picks), Bet Max Longshot (underdog wins pay a bonus), Bet Max Streaks (win streaks unlock payout multipliers), Bet Max Bracket (single-elimination head-to-head), Bet Max Survivor (one pick per round, one miss and you're out), and Bet Max Squads (team up and rank together).
